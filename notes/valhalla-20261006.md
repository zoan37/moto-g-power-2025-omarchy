### Recovery and slot limits

Lenovo's actual rescue recipe for this phone waits for fastboot, checks the device match and executes the firmware XML. Its tool resource is `Vegas_fastboot_flashtool.zip`; no separate download agent is present in the stock ZIP. The recipe is not evidence of recovery when LK failure removes fastboot access.

The actual phone is on slot B and has slotted LK. The candidate and newer official XML target `lk_a`; no slot switch is selected. Before any experimental write, establish explicit targeting and restoration limits. Do not treat the other slot as a fallback without evidence of its contents and boot-chain behavior.

A [published firsthand Val Protocol procedure for another MT6835 Motorola](https://github.com/s0meguy1/kansas-unlock#recovery-paths), XT2513V `kansas`, reports unlock success but warns that losing fastboot leaves no established practical BROM rescue. This is related-device evidence, not verification for Vegas. [MTKClient issue 22](https://github.com/bkerler/mtkclient/issues/22) reports a patched V6 device and difficulty reaching bootrom; the issue's closed status alone does not establish a usable recovery method for this phone.

**Assessment:** The metadata mismatch can potentially be resolved without changing the installed OS by using the exact-build candidate. Cross-channel applicability still needs a defensible decision, and low-level recovery remains unverified. Nothing found makes flashing a proven, recoverable operation on this unit. Waiting for official OEM unlocking remains the route that avoids replacing LK. If the candidate is pursued, review its current-build manifest and concrete slot/restoration procedure before authorizing a phone write.
