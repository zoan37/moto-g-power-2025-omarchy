# Vibration (2026-10-11)

- Hardware: **Awinic AW862xx** linear resonant actuator on I2C `11-005a` (DT `haptic_nv@5A`, `awinic,haptic_nv`,
  status okay; the DT's `regulator-vibrator` is disabled). Driver: vendor_dlkm `aw862x_haptic_nv_v1.ko` (no deps),
  waveform files `aw862x_haptic.bin` / `aw862xx_haptic.bin` from the vendor partition into `/usr/lib/firmware`
  (kept under `artifacts/haptics/`, gitignored). The `aw862xx_rtp_*.bin` ringtone-synced patterns aren't installed.
- At probe the driver calibrates the resonance itself: `f0 = 2082` (208 Hz; factory window 190.6–219.3 Hz).
- Interface: `/sys/class/leds/vibrator`. Write milliseconds to `duration`, then `1` to `activate`; short durations
  play from the waveform RAM. Writes return in ~0.03 ms (the driver queues the work).
- `vegas-haptics.service` (`haptics-prepare`) loads the driver and makes duration/activate/state/index/
  activate_mode/gain writable by group `input` (the desktop user). Opt out with `/var/lib/vegas/no-haptics`.
- Users of it:
  - `vegas-vibrate [tick|notify|long|MS]`: 15 ms tick; notify = two 45 ms pulses; `~/.config/vegas/haptics` = `off`
    silences it
  - the keyboard: a 12 ms tick on every key press, built into wvkbd by `scripts/build-vegas-phone-keyboard.py`
    (`VEGAS_KEYBOARD_HAPTIC_MS`, 0 = off)
  - the power key's long press (power menu): a tick
  - notifications: Omarchy patch `30-notification-vibrate.patch`. A popup that shows vibrates (`notify`); nothing
    vibrates under Do Not Disturb, for ephemeral senders (Omarchy action toasts, bare `notify-send`) or at low urgency.
